LESSON NOTES

Start Learning

1. Grammar

Grammar is the set of rules that helps us arrange words correctly when speaking or writing.

Example: She reads every day.

In this sentence, "She" is the subject, "reads" is the verb, and "every day" tells us when the action happens.

2. Vocabulary

Vocabulary means the words we know and use. Learning new words helps us communicate better.

Example: "Happy" means feeling pleasure or joy.

A synonym for happy is "joyful". An antonym is "sad".

3. Reading Comprehension

Reading comprehension means understanding what you read.

When reading a passage, look for its main idea, important details, and the meaning of unfamiliar words in context.

4. Essay Writing

An essay usually contains an introduction, a body, and a conclusion.

The introduction presents the topic. The body explains your ideas, and the conclusion brings the main points together.

Keep improving your English.

Consistent reading, writing, and practice can help you become a more confident communicator.
