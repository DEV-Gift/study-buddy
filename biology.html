Explore Biology Topics

Start with the basics and build your knowledge.

🔬 1. Cell Biology

Cells are the basic structural and functional units of living organisms. Some organisms consist of one cell, while others contain many cells.

Key point: Cells carry out the processes needed for life.

🌱 2. Plant Biology

Plants are living organisms. Most plants make their own food through photosynthesis, using light energy, water, and carbon dioxide.

Key point: Chlorophyll helps plants absorb light energy.

🫀 3. Human Biology

Human biology examines the structure and function of the human body, including organs and body systems such as the digestive and respiratory systems.

Key point: Different organs work together to keep the body functioning.

🐾 4. Ecology

Ecology is the study of how organisms interact with one another and with their environment.

Key point: Living things depend on their environment for resources.

🧬 5. Genetics

Genetics is the study of heredity and variation. Genes carry information that can be passed from parents to offspring.

Key point: Genes influence inherited characteristics.

🦠 6. Microbiology

Microbiology studies microscopic organisms, including bacteria and many other organisms that cannot be seen clearly with the naked eye.

Key point: Microorganisms live in many different environments.
