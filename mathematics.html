1. Basic Arithmetic

Arithmetic is the branch of mathematics that deals with numbers and basic mathematical operations.

The four basic operations

Addition (+): Combining two or more numbers.

Example: 5 + 3 = 8

Subtraction (-): Taking one number away from another.

Example: 10 - 4 = 6

Multiplication (×): Repeated addition.

Example: 4 × 3 = 12

Division (÷): Sharing a number into equal groups.

Example: 12 ÷ 3 = 4

2. Algebra

Algebra is a branch of mathematics that uses letters and symbols to represent unknown numbers.

Understanding variables

A variable is a letter that represents a number. Common examples are x, y and z.

Example: x + 5 = 10

To find x, subtract 5 from both sides.

x = 10 - 5

x = 5

3. Geometry

Geometry is the study of shapes, sizes, angles and the properties of objects.

Area of a rectangle

Area is the amount of space inside a flat shape.

Formula: Area = Length × Width

Example: Length = 6 cm, Width = 4 cm

Area = 6 × 4

Area = 24 cm²

Perimeter of a rectangle

Perimeter is the total distance around a shape.

Formula: Perimeter = 2 × (Length + Width)

Example: 2 × (6 + 4) = 20 cm

4. Statistics

Statistics involves collecting, organizing, analysing and interpreting data.

Understanding the mean

The mean is the average of a group of numbers. Add all the numbers together and divide by how many numbers there are.

Numbers: 2, 4, 6, 8

Total = 2 + 4 + 6 + 8 = 20

Number of values = 4

Mean = 20 ÷ 4

Mean = 5
